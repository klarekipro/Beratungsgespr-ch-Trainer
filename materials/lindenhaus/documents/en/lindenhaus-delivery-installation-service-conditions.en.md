# Service conditions

## Delivery and timing

Delivery within the city and up to 20 km. Clarify access and floor before confirmation. In stock: 5–10 working days after technical clarification. On order: approximately 3–5 weeks after approval. Monday to Friday 08:00–12:00 or 13:00–17:00. Exact appointments are not recorded; requested dates are not bookings. No immediate or same-day service. Outside the training area, clarify feasibility and costs separately. Stock figures apply only to this training.

## Installation and compatibility

All dimensions: width x height x depth in cm. Appliance dimensions are not niche dimensions. Before a binding order, check connections, ventilation, clearance and installation for the specific model. Ovens: electric connection, no gas; a qualified professional must check the connection. Niche values are training values, not general installation guidance. Dishwashers: Eco, Normal and Intensive; check furniture front, plinth and connections for integrated models. Washing machines: electric front loaders for private households. Connection requires existing accessible suitable connections. Hose extensions, alterations, under-counter fitting and new furniture fronts are excluded.

## Costs and payment

Prices in EUR include tax; services cost extra unless expressly included. No current promotions or free extras. Do not charge installation and connection twice for the same appliance. Delivery is charged once per order. A site inspection is separately commissioned, with no automatic credit. Advice and a non-binding quotation are free. Request payment only after explicit order approval; collect no real payment details during training. Payment processing is outside the role play. No goods or appointment reservations and no advance payment for consulting another decision maker.

## Quotation and order approval

Before a quotation, clarify needs, key criteria, technical suitability or pending checks, total budget including services, timing and decision makers. Summarise needs and give a reasoned recommendation; do not select solely for a higher price. A written quotation includes product ID, known features, services, total price, pending checks and delivery status; valid for 7 calendar days on this catalogue version, without reservation. An order requires explicit acceptance of the specific quotation, resolved technical questions and consent of authorised decision makers. Interest, a drink or discussion with a partner is not an order. Mark an open decision as quotation for review or decision pending consultation. After acceptance the appointment may remain open; do not claim delivery or installation has happened.

## Diagnosis and repair

First clarify appliance type, use, symptoms, history and expectations; do not diagnose an unconfirmed cause. Diagnosis: 90 EUR for supported electric household appliances only. Repair, parts and a replacement appliance are excluded; no success guarantee. Repair requires diagnosis and a separate quotation with consent. Do not sell standard paid diagnosis for gas or industrial appliances. Do not invent a partner company; asking the manufacturer for a suitable service is a possible next step, not a referral promise.

## Limits and questions requiring clarification

Offer only catalogue prices and defined services; collect open questions and agree a follow-up. Do not invent discounts, promotions, free installation, fixed appointments or repair success; exceptions require documented approval. Unrecorded information is unknown. Do not infer energy ratings, consumption, programme durations or voluntary warranties from price, brand or capacity. Refer legal rights and manufacturer warranties to the responsible party; invent no duration or coverage. These training rules are not complete terms and conditions or legal advice. Respect breaks, comparison quotations and shared decisions. Drinks must have no purchase condition; no personal or family pressure. Summarise open questions and the total price, arrange follow-up with consent only. Do not claim fictional contact details have been collected.
